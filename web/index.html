What it does

  • Chat history and live messages. Collect past posts in a date range, then keep listening for new ones.
  • Keyword filters. Match any or all keywords, with case-sensitive and whole-word options. Hebrew and emoji work.
  • Text and links. Captures inline links, hidden links, link previews and buttons.
  • Excel export. A summary sheet, an all-messages sheet and one sheet per channel, with timestamps, sources and direct message links.

Privacy

  • This site is static. Your browser talks only to Telegram.
  • Without "Remember me", your login is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • With it, your login is stored on this device only, AES-256 encrypted with your passphrase.
